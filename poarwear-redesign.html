Our Origin

Born from a
missed train.

Rosa Aurora Oviedo Polanco spent years navigating New York City's subways and buses as a Sales Territory Manager. Every morning, the same frustration: digging through her bag for a MetroCard as doors threatened to close. After trying every bag on the market, she realized none were truly designed for city life. So she designed one herself.
